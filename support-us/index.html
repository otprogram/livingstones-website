Support our cause financially

The value of sharing and preserving Jewish heritage and to foster intercultural dialogue can’t begin without your support. You have the power to provide education and directly inspire future generations to understand the complex world they live in. No matter how much you choose to give, your generosity will 100% directly fund LivingStones projects and those who need it most.

  • Restore and preserve historical Jewish sites, ensuring their significance endures.
  • Create educational resources that promote cultural appreciation and awareness.
  • Facilitate intercultural dialogues that bridge divides and foster unity.
  • Provide direct support to local Jewish communities, nurturing their growth.
  • Organize meaningful events that celebrate our shared history and heritage.

As you consider your donation, know that your generosity is an investment in a future where cultural understanding thrives, where history is remembered, and where communities come together to build a more inclusive world.

We deeply appreciate your support, and each contribution takes us a step closer to achieving our goals. Your donation is more than just financial; it's a commitment to a world where unity, appreciation, and understanding reign.

Donation Levels and Benefits

  • Supporter (Up to CHF 49) - Personalized thank you email
  • Contributor (CHF 50 - CHF 99) - Exclusive periodic newsletter
  • Advocate (CHF 100 - CHF 249) - Invitation to virtual events and webinars
  • Champion (CHF 250 - CHF 499) - Recognition in our annual report
  • Patron (CHF 500 - CHF 999) - A limited-edition merchandise item (e.g., tote bag, mug)
  • Benefactor (CHF 1,000 - CHF 4,999) - Personalized video message from a board member
  • Visionary (CHF 5,000 and above) - Invitation to an exclusive event with prominent speakers

Each level includes all the previous benefits.
