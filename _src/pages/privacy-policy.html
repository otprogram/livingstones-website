Privacy Policy

The LivingStones Association, Switzerland, is responsible for this website. Contact: info@livingstones-association.org.

What we collect

We only collect the details you send us through the contact, newsletter and donation-notice forms (for example your name, email address and message). The forms are delivered to our mailbox through the service FormSubmit. We use these details only to answer you, to send you our updates if you signed up, and to thank you for donations. We do not sell or share them.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Embedded YouTube videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and maps are provided by OpenStreetMap; these services may process your data under their own policies when you use them.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time which data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, or to remove you from our mailing list, by writing to info@livingstones-association.org.
