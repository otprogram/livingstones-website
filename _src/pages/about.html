About LivingStones

LivingStones is a Non-Profit-Organization which was founded in Switzerland with the initiative of Yvette Merzbacher, a Peru-born Jewish philanthropist, genealogist, and writer who traces her roots back to Bessarabia (today's Moldova) and Germany. LivingStones has grown considerably over the years through its various initiatives thanks to the hard work, creativity, and passionate efforts of its board members and the excellent partnerships established with local and international NGOs and organizations.

Education is fundamental for us and an integral part of the LivingStones projects. We provide leadership and informal Jewish education to youngsters to break stereotypes and prejudice when speaking about Jewish heritage and culture in schools to foster a strong and vibrant Jewish life and enhance cultural identity. We build memorials to learn about the Holocaust, protect Jewish cemeteries, and promote Jewish films and artists.

We believe that by educating the youth about the Holocaust and promoting dialogue between different faiths, we can create a world free of hate, discrimination, and prejudice.

Our Mission

We inspire, educate and resonate with our audience highlighting the importance of commemorating, preserving, and sharing our Jewish heritage. Our efforts contribute to fostering understanding and dialogue among people from all walks of life. Our unwavering commitment is to foster cultural connections and promoting unity through shared history and heritage.

Our Vision

We envision a safe world where the echoes of Jewish heritage resonate deeply with individuals from all walks of life.
